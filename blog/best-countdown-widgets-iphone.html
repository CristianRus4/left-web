Best Countdown Widgets for iPhone Home Screen (2026)

Updated April 2026 · 8 min read

A good countdown widget turns your iPhone Home Screen into a visual reminder of the things you care about most. Whether you're tracking days until a vacation, a birthday, a wedding, or just the end of the year, the right countdown widget for iPhone keeps that number front and center without needing to open an app. But with dozens of options on the App Store, which ones are actually worth installing?

We tested and compared the best countdown widget iOS apps available in 2026, evaluating design quality, widget variety, customization, and pricing. Here are the five that stood out.

What Makes a Good Countdown Widget?

Before diving in, here's what we looked for when evaluating each app:

  • Widget design — Does it look good on your Home Screen? Clean typography and balanced layout matter more than you might think.
  • Widget variety — Can you choose different sizes and styles? Small, medium, large, Lock Screen?
  • Customization — Can you change colors, fonts, backgrounds, and what data is displayed?
  • Extras — Does the app offer anything beyond basic countdowns, like habit tracking, progress bars, or life widgets?
  • Pricing — Is there a free tier? Is it a subscription or a one-time purchase?

The 5 Best Countdown Widget Apps for iPhone

1. Left

Free, one-time unlock from $0.99 · cntxt

Left combines countdown widgets with habit tracking, days-since counters, year progress, and life visualization into a single, beautifully designed app. With over 3,000 widget combinations across different sizes, styles, and data types, it offers one of the broadest widget libraries of any app in this category. The design is intentionally minimal — clean typography, muted tones, and a focus on information density without clutter.

Where Left really differentiates itself is in combining features that would normally require two or three separate apps. Your countdown widgets live alongside habit streak trackers, "days since" counters, and even a life progress widget that visualizes your time in weeks. The one-time purchase model is also increasingly rare, and a welcome change from the subscription fatigue in this space.

That said, Left's design is opinionated. The minimal aesthetic won't appeal to everyone — if you want bright colors, custom backgrounds, or playful layouts, you may find it too restrained. It's also iPhone-only, so Android users are out of luck.

Pros

  • 3000+ widget combinations across all sizes
  • Habit tracker, days-since counter, and life widgets included
  • Year progress visualization built in
  • One-time purchase — no subscription
  • Exceptionally clean design quality

Cons

  • Opinionated minimal aesthetic — not for everyone
  • No Android version
  • No custom photo backgrounds on widgets

2. Pretty Progress

Free with subscription · keinois OU

Pretty Progress takes a focused approach to countdowns by presenting them as visual progress bars. Instead of just showing "42 days left," it shows you how far through a period you are — which can be a more intuitive way to understand the passage of time. The app is simple, well-designed, and popular in the countdown category for good reason.

The simplicity is both its strength and its limitation. If all you want is a clean progress bar on your Home Screen showing how far you are through the year, a semester, or a project deadline, Pretty Progress does that well. But there's no habit tracking, no days-since counting, and widget customization is more limited than some competitors. The subscription pricing model also makes it harder to justify for a single-purpose utility.

Pros

  • Simple, focused concept — progress bars done well
  • Clean visual design
  • Popular and well-maintained
  • Easy to set up quickly

Cons

  • Subscription pricing for a single-purpose app
  • No habit tracking or days-since features
  • Limited widget customization options

3. Countdown Widget & Counter

Free with ads, premium unlock · The Good App Company

If granularity is what you're after, Countdown Widget & Counter delivers. It can display countdowns in days, hours, minutes, and even seconds — making it one of the most detailed countdown apps available. You can organize events into categories, and there's a good variety of widget sizes to choose from. For tracking multiple events with precise timing, it's a strong option.

The trade-off is that the free tier is ad-supported, and the interface can feel busy compared to more minimal alternatives. There's a lot packed into the app, which is great for power users but can be overwhelming if you just want a single clean widget on your Home Screen. The premium unlock removes ads and opens up additional features.

Pros

  • Granular countdown options (days, hours, minutes, seconds)
  • Good widget variety across sizes
  • Event categories for organization
  • Feature-rich for power users

Cons

  • Ad-supported free tier
  • Interface can feel cluttered
  • Design quality is functional rather than refined

4. one year: countdown widget

Free with premium · Wind Down Studio

one year is the closest aesthetic peer to Left on this list. It takes a minimal, intentional approach to visualizing the passage of the year — showing your progress through the current year with beautiful, understated design. If you appreciate quiet design and just want a tasteful year-progress widget, one year is worth a look.

The limitation is right there in the name: it's focused almost exclusively on the year. There's no general-purpose countdown for arbitrary dates, no habit tracking, and no days-since counters. It does one thing and does it with care, but if you need more flexibility, you'll need a second app alongside it.

Pros

  • Beautiful, minimal aesthetic
  • Thoughtful year-progress visualization
  • Closest design peer to Left
  • Lightweight and focused

Cons

  • Very limited feature set — year-focused only
  • No general-purpose countdowns for arbitrary dates
  • No habit tracking or days-since features

5. Widgetsmith

Free with premium subscription · Cross Forward Consulting

Widgetsmith is the Swiss Army knife of iPhone widgets. It supports countdowns alongside weather, photos, calendars, health data, astronomy, and dozens of other widget types. The customization depth is unmatched — you can control fonts, colors, backgrounds, borders, and tinting for almost every element. If you want total control over how your Home Screen looks, Widgetsmith gives you more knobs to turn than any other app here.

The downside is that countdowns are just one of many features, not the focus. Setting up a countdown widget requires navigating a complex, sometimes confusing interface. The app has grown significantly over the years, and the UI reflects that accumulated complexity. It's also subscription-based, which is a lot to pay if countdowns are your primary use case.

Pros

  • Massive widget customization options
  • Many widget types beyond countdowns
  • Very popular with a large community
  • Granular control over design elements

Cons

  • Countdowns are not the primary focus
  • Complex UI — steep learning curve
  • Subscription pricing model
  • Can feel bloated for single-purpose use

The Verdict

Our Verdict

There's no single best countdown widget app — it depends on what you value. Pretty Progress is the simplest and most focused if all you want is a progress bar. Countdown Widget & Counter is best for granular, to-the-second countdowns across multiple events. one year is a beautiful, minimal choice if year progress is all you need. Widgetsmith offers the most raw customization across many widget types.

Left stands out for combining countdowns with habit tracking, days-since counters, year progress, and life widgets in a single, well-designed app — all with a one-time purchase instead of a subscription. If you want one app that replaces several and you appreciate minimal design, it's the strongest overall pick. But if Left's aesthetic is too restrained for your taste, Pretty Progress or Widgetsmith are solid alternatives with different design philosophies.
